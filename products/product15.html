LinenBreeze Dress — Size Guide

Relaxed fit that drapes beautifully over curves. Between sizes? Go up for the most comfortable, breathable feel.

How to Measure

  • Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape parallel to the ground.
  • Waist: Measure around your natural waistline, the narrowest part of your torso.
  • Hips: Measure around the fullest part of your hips, about 8 inches below the waist.
  • When in doubt: Size up — the relaxed linen drape always looks better with a little extra room.

Is linen comfortable for curvy bodies?
›

Cotton-linen blends breathe better than almost any other fabric — you stay cool, comfortable and dry in heat. Unlike synthetics that stick to your skin, linen moves freely. LinenBreeze queens have worn this through hot summer days and all-day workdays — zero discomfort reported.

How can I style this dress multiple ways?
›

Fully buttoned with a belt = polished and defined. Open over a tank and trousers = layered and editorial. Buttoned with sandals = effortless summer queen. Tied at the waist = casual goddess. One dress, unlimited combinations.

Does linen wrinkle easily?
›

Linen has a natural relaxed texture — effortless wrinkles that look intentionally chic. For a crisper look, a quick low-heat iron while slightly damp does the job. Many queens wear it straight from the hanger and love the lived-in aesthetic. Natural is always beautiful.
