Can I use this essence if I have active acne breakouts?
›

Yes — propolis has direct antibacterial properties that help with active acne and the marks left behind. If you are using prescription acne treatments, allow 10 minutes between applications.

Can I use this under my sunscreen and makeup?
›

Absolutely. Apply first on clean skin, wait 60 seconds, then apply moisturizer and SPF. The lightweight texture absorbs completely without residue.

Does this work for dark skin tones and melanin-rich complexions?
›

Yes — designed with all skin tones in mind. Nicotinamide works specifically on the melanin transfer process, making it highly effective for darker complexions where post-acne marks are most visible.

How long does one bottle last?

With 3 to 5 drops twice daily, one bottle provides approximately 4 to 6 weeks of consistent use. Consider the bundle options for uninterrupted results.
