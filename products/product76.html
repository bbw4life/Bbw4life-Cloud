PatchLace Flats — Size Guide

Designed to be light on the foot, easy on the eye. Measure heel to toe. When between sizes, go up for a relaxed, breathable fit — the soft patchwork upper flexes with every step.

How to Measure Your Foot

  • Step 1: Place your foot flat on a piece of paper.
  • Step 2: Mark the tip of your longest toe and the back of your heel.
  • Step 3: Measure the distance in cm.
  • When between sizes, choose the larger size for max comfort.
