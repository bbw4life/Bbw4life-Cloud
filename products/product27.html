RuffleOne — Size Guide

Measure your bust and hips for best fit. If between sizes, size up — a relaxed fit drapes the ruffles more beautifully.

How to Measure

  • Bust: Around the fullest part of your chest.
  • Waist: Around your natural waistline.
  • Hips: Around the fullest part of your hips.
  • Between sizes? Always choose the larger size.
