Do I need to rinse after using the pads?
›

No rinsing required. After wiping, skin is clean and ready for the next step. Queens who prefer to rinse can, but it is not necessary.

How many pads do I need per cleanse?
›

One pad is enough for a full face. For heavy or waterproof eye/lip makeup, use a second pad for those areas specifically.

Can I use these every day, twice a day?
›

Yes — designed for daily morning and evening use. Queens who use them twice daily consistently report the most visible improvement in skin tone and clarity.

Will lemon in the formula cause photosensitivity?
›

The lemon is safe for daily use at our concentration. Apply SPF in the morning after use if in direct sun — standard practice for any brightening product.
