StripedMini Dress — Size Guide

Flattering V-neck, body-skimming fit with gentle stretch. Between sizes? Go up for the most comfortable fit.

How to Measure

  • Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your chest, tape parallel to the ground.
  • Waist: Measure around your natural waistline, the narrowest part of your torso.
  • Hips: Measure around the fullest part of your hips, about 8 inches below the waist.
  • When in doubt: Size up — the stretch fabric drapes better with a little extra room.
