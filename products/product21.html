CurveBikini — Size Guide

High-waist bottom with full coverage and support. Between sizes? Size up for maximum comfort.

How to Measure Your Body

  • Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape parallel to the floor.
  • Waist: Measure around the narrowest part of your natural waist.
  • Hips: Measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat.
  • When between sizes, always choose the larger size for maximum comfort and the most flattering high-waist silhouette.

Does the black color fade after washing or pool use?
›

The CurveBikini uses colorfast fabric engineered to resist fading from chlorine, salt water, and regular washing. Rinse in cool fresh water after each use, hand wash gently, and dry flat away from direct sunlight. Follow this routine and your black bikini stays striking season after season.

How does the sizing run — should I size up?
›

The CurveBikini runs true to size. If you are between sizes, size up — especially for the bottom, so the high-waist sits comfortably at your natural waist. Your comfort creates your confidence. Available from XL to 4XL.
