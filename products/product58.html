Will the warming sensation be too strong for sensitive lips?
›

Gentle and soothing — not intense. Women with sensitive lips consistently report that the warmth feels comforting. The formula is designed to care for every woman, regardless of skin sensitivity.

How quickly will I see results on my lip lines?
›

Softer, more hydrated lips after the first application. Visible improvement in lip lines within 5 to 7 days of consistent morning and evening use. The overnight treatment is where the most noticeable transformation happens.

Can I apply lip color over it?
›

Yes. Non-greasy finish — perfect base for lip color. Allow 1–2 minutes to absorb, then apply gloss or lipstick. Creates a smoother base that makes color last longer.
