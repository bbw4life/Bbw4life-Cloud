IrregularTop — Size Guide

Loose fit — drapes generously. Measure your bust for accurate sizing. Size up for an even more relaxed silhouette.

How to Measure

  • Bust: Around the fullest part of your chest, tape parallel to the floor.
  • Waist: At your natural waistline.
  • Hips: Around the fullest part of your hips.
  • Between sizes? Choose the larger size for a flowy drape.

Does the loose fit mean it will look too big on me?
›

Not at all. The loose silhouette drapes over curves — not swallows them. The irregular hem creates shape, the round neck frames you beautifully. Half-tuck or knot at the waist for extra definition.

I'm between sizes. Should I size up or down?
›

Size up for a more relaxed, flowy drape. Your usual size for a more structured bust fit. Either works — check the size chart above.

Is the irregular hem noticeable or subtle?
›

Intentionally noticeable. The asymmetric hem is the design — it creates movement, attitude and is the first thing people notice.
