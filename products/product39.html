BritishLoafers — Men's Formal Shoe Size Guide

Fits true to size. Between sizes? Choose the larger size for maximum comfort throughout the day.

How to Measure Your Foot

  • Length: Stand on a flat surface and measure from your heel to the tip of your longest toe.
  • Width: Measure the widest part of your foot across the ball.
  • Time of day: Measure in the evening — feet naturally swell slightly during the day.
  • Between sizes? Choose the larger size for dress shoes — comfort is king.

Will these loafers be comfortable enough to wear all day at a wedding or event?
›

Yes. The slip-on design eliminates pressure points from laces, and the construction provides a stable, supportive base throughout the day. Whether at the altar, the reception or a full-day conference — these loafers carry you from start to finish.

Which color should I start with?
›

Black is the most universal — pairs with every suit. Brown is the most versatile across semi-formal occasions. Blue is for the king who wants to stand out. Start with Black. Then Brown. Then you'll understand why you need all three.

What trousers pair best with the BritishLoafers?
›

Slim or straight-cut trousers finishing just above the ankle. Charcoal or navy suit trousers with Black loafers is timeless. Beige or olive chinos with Brown is a perfect smart-casual look. Avoid baggy trousers — the BritishLoafers deserve to be seen.

Do these loafers fit wider feet?
›

The BritishLoafers accommodate wider feet better than most dress shoes. If you have a wider foot, size up half a size. The slip-on construction removes instep pressure points. Kings plus size consistently report a comfortable fit through full-day events.
