How to Use It & Why It Works
Apply to scalp & lengths 3–5 drops on fingertips, massage in circular motions for 2–3 minutes, then distribute through mid-lengths and ends.
Daily leave-in or intensive treatment Use 2–3 drops daily as a leave-in, or apply generously and cover 30–60 minutes before shampooing for intensive repair.
Ancient formula, real penetration Batana's fatty acid profile penetrates the hair cortex to rebuild moisture from within — a Miskito tradition proven over generations.
Does batana oil work on relaxed and chemically processed hair?
›

Yes. BatanaGlow is particularly beneficial for relaxed hair. It rebuilds the lipid layer stripped by chemicals, reducing brittleness and moisture loss. Queens report dramatically improved manageability and reduced breakage within 4–6 weeks.

Will batana oil make my hair greasy?
›

No — when used in the correct amount. Batana penetrates rather than coats. Start with 3 drops. Fine hair: 1–2 drops on ends only. If any heaviness, reduce the amount.

Can I use BatanaGlow Oil for hair growth?
›

Yes. Scalp application improves circulation and nourishes follicles. Creates the optimal environment for full growth potential. Most improvement seen in 6–8 weeks of consistent use.

Is BatanaGlow Oil safe for colored or bleached hair?
›

It is one of the best treatments for color-damaged hair. Restores the lipid layer, improves cuticle integrity and extends color vibrancy by sealing the cuticle.
