How to Find Your Perfect Bra Size
1
Measure Your Band Tape under bust, parallel to floor. Round to nearest even number.
2
Measure Your Bust Around the fullest part, tape relaxed and parallel.
3
Calculate Your Cup Bust minus band. Each 2.5cm = one cup size.
4
Between Sizes? Size up for best comfort.
Queen tip: Measure at end of day. Berline in the chat can also help.
Is this bra comfortable for full-figured women all day?
›

Yes — breathable mesh keeps you cool during long days. Wide straps prevent shoulder dig-in. Queens who wear this describe it as the first bra in years that didn't hurt. Gathered cups provide lift without underwire pressure.

How do I find my correct size in this bra?
›

Measure band (under bust) and bust (fullest part). The difference determines your cup size. When in doubt, size up. A slightly larger cup with a firm band gives the most comfortable result for full figures. Our Berline assistant in the chat can help too.

Does this bra have underwire?
›

No — support comes entirely from the gathered mesh cups, wide band and adjustable straps. A deliberate choice for plus size comfort — underwire in larger cups creates pressure that builds all day. Most queens who switch never go back.
