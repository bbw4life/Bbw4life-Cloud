HikeJacket — Men's Size Guide

The HikeJacket is cut generously for full range of movement and layering. If between sizes, go up for best outdoor comfort.

How to Measure

  • Chest: Measure around the fullest part of your chest, under your arms.
  • Waist: Measure around your natural waistline.
  • For layering underneath, size up for best fit and freedom of movement.
  • When between sizes, always choose the larger size.

›

The HikeJacket is a waterproof wind-resistant outer shell. For cold weather, layer a fleece underneath — the jacket stops wind and rain while your inner layer traps heat. In mild conditions, it works alone.

Machine wash cold, gentle cycle — hang dry only. No fabric softener, no tumble dry. Both degrade the waterproof coating. With proper care, this jacket protects you through many adventures.
