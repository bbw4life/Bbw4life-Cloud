How long do BowNails hold?
+

With NailBond Glue: 5–10 days on clean, oil-free nails. With adhesive tabs: 1–3 days. Key factors: clean nails, proper sizing, tip flush against the cuticle with no gaps. These three steps are what separate press-ons that last from ones that lift by day two.

Butterfly Gothic or Dark Red Bow?
+

Butterfly Gothic — bold graphic art, darker tones, immediate statement. For queens who wear their edge with confidence.

Dark Red Bow — deep red + elegant bow, romantic power without being loud. Many queens order both — one for daily boldness, one for special occasions.

Will these nails fit and look good with a fuller hand shape?
›

Beautiful hands come in every shape and size. The long almond tips are cut to elongate the fingers and flatter every hand — full, slender, wide, it doesn't matter. Confidence is the only accessory these nails need.

Do I need salon experience to apply these myself?
›

Not at all. No salon, no appointment, no waiting. With glue or adhesive tabs, you're fully done in about 10 minutes at home. Salon-level glam, on your own time.

Can I really wear press-ons every day without damaging my natural nails?
›

Yes. Removed gently, these tips protect rather than harm. Reusable, non-damaging, and ready whenever you are — Butterfly Gothic for bold days, Dark Red Bow for romantic ones. Your hands, your rules.
