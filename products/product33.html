Why are these shorts different for plus size queens?
+

The DalmationShorts were not made by scaling up a regular-size pattern. The high waist is structurally designed to sit correctly on a plus-size body — sitting at the natural waist rather than folding or rolling. The fabric is chosen to have enough stretch to accommodate curves without losing its shape throughout the day.

The Dalmatian print itself was selected because high-contrast bold prints on curvy bodies create a powerful visual presence. This is not a compromise version of a short. It is designed from the beginning for queens in US sizes 14 to 24. Every stitch, every seam, every proportional choice was made with your body in mind — not adjusted after the fact.

Do the shorts run true to size?
›

The DalmationShorts follow standard plus US sizing (1X = US 14–16, 2X = US 18–20, 3X = US 22–24). If you are between sizes or prefer a looser feel, size up. Use the size chart on this page for the most accurate fit.

Will the Dalmatian print fade after washing?
›

Wash inside out in cold water, gentle cycle, hang to dry. The high-contrast white and black is resilient — designed to look just as bold after many washes as when they first arrived.

Can I wear these beyond summer?
›

Absolutely. In cooler weather, pair with opaque black tights and ankle boots. Dalmatian never goes out of style — many queens wear them well into autumn.
